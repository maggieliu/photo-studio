Privacy Policy

Photo Studio · Last updated November 6, 2026

In short: your photos stay on your device. Photo Studio has no accounts, no server that receives your photos, no ads and no analytics.

What Photo Studio is

Photo Studio is a photo editor and catalog that runs in your web browser, including the Pi Browser. You can import photos, organize them into albums with tags and ratings, edit them, and export them.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps this in your browser's storage for this website. It never leaves your device:

  • The photos you import, plus small preview pictures made from them.
  • Your edits, albums, tags, ratings and favorites.
  • A few settings, such as your last export choices and whether you have seen the welcome page.
  • If you choose to sign in with Pi, your Pi username and a Pi user ID for this app (see below).

You can remove photos inside the app at any time. Clearing this website's data in your browser settings removes everything the app has stored.

What is sent to anyone

  • Your photos: never uploaded. Editing and exporting happen on your device. When you export, the files are saved or shared by you, using your device's own share or download feature.
  • Hosting: the app's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may record basic technical information about visits, such as your IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement. Photo Studio itself does not receive or store this.
  • Sign in with Pi (optional): only if you tap the button, the app loads Pi's sign-in script from sdk.minepi.com and asks Pi for your username. Pi then handles the sign-in under its own terms and privacy policy. Photo Studio saves your username and user ID on your device to show who is signed in. It does not keep the access token Pi returns, does not read your wallet, and does not make payments. Signing out removes the saved details.
  • Analytics and advertising: none. There are no tracking scripts or advertising partners.

Cookies

Photo Studio does not use cookies. It uses your browser's local storage as described above.

Children

Photo Studio is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

  • Delete photos, albums and edits inside the app.
  • Sign out of Pi from the sidebar.
  • Clear this website's data in your browser settings to remove everything stored on your device.

Because the app stores everything on your device only, we cannot recover photos for you. Keep copies of anything you care about.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Using the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Questions
