## Name
Photo Studio

## Tagline (short)
Edit and organize your photos. Private, on your device.

## Category
Photography if the console offers it; otherwise keep Education.
(The app is currently set to Education.)

## Description
Photo Studio is a private photo editor and catalog that runs entirely in your browser.

- Import photos from your phone or computer
- Organize with albums, tags, favorites and star ratings
- Edit with light, color, detail, crop and presets
- Non-destructive: your original is never changed, and you can revert any time
- Export as JPEG, PNG or WebP
- Works on phone and desktop

Your photos stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to create.
Signing in with Pi is optional.
